Moving subject

Try 1/500 s for a moving person; use 1/1000 s or faster for quicker action. These are starting points, not guarantees. Raise ISO or find more light if the image is too dark.

  1. Use shutter-priority (Tv) and choose a speed. Try continuous shooting and follow the subject smoothly.
  2. On the R50, set AF operation to Servo AF and hold the shutter halfway while following the subject. Check a burst at full size for sharp eyes and motion blur.
  3. If blurred, try a faster shutter; if focus misses, keep the subject in the AF area. Switch back for still subjects.

R50 controls: Canon AF operation and AF area. M50 menus and controls have not been verified for this card.

Before a photo outing

  • Charge a battery; pack a spare and a memory card with room.
  • Choose a lens for the distance and light; bring a cloth and weather protection.
  • Check the forecast, destination access and light. Download this library before leaving.
  • Take a test frame; check focus, shutter speed, exposure and remaining card space. Reset any unusual settings afterward.

RØDE NT-USB Mini

Connect the mic by USB to the computer; select it as the input in the recording app. Plug headphones into the mic's rear 3.5 mm output and turn the front knob for headphone volume. Record and listen to a short test before a long take.

Headphone volume is not the recording gain; check the app's input meter. Source: RØDE quick-start guide. This workflow has not been field-tested for this card.
